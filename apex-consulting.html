The Problem

Apex Consulting had an outdated web presence that failed to reflect their enterprise consulting reputation, lacked a secure way to share confidential project deliverables with clients, and suffered low lead conversion rates.

"Our previous web presence didn't convey our enterprise standing or provide a secure medium for client report delivery."

The System We Built

High-Converting Corporate Showcase

Modern, responsive visual identity with interactive service showcases and strategic lead intake funnels designed for corporate decision-makers.

Built on Next.js with SSG for ultra-fast load times and SEO rankings.

Secure Client Portal

Protected document exchange and project milestone tracking dashboard for corporate clients with granular access permissions.

Encrypted cloud storage integration with role-based auth.

Headless CMS for Content Marketing

Custom headless CMS setup allowing non-technical staff to publish market reports and case studies effortlessly.

Sanity CMS integration with preview mode and structured content schema.

Sub-second load speeds, 98+ Lighthouse scores, and full compliance for client data handling.

Edge caching, automated image optimization, and strict security headers.

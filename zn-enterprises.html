Case Study Interior Design & AI Automation

ZN Enterprises

An AI-assisted bill of quantities generator for an interior design firm.

10x Faster Estimation Speed
Structured AI Material Breakdown
Unified Client Intake Pipeline

The Problem

ZN Enterprises, an interior design firm, needed a faster way to generate bills of quantities (BOQs) for client projects. Manually itemizing materials, quantities, and costs for every project was slow and inconsistent between team members.

"Manually compiling detailed material cost breakdowns for large interior builds consumed days of engineering overhead."

The System We Built

AI Powered BOQ Generation

Instead of manually building cost breakdowns from scratch for every project, the system generates structured bills of quantities using AI, cutting down the time needed to produce accurate estimates.

AI integration generating structured, itemized output from project inputs.

Client Requirements Intake

A structured intake process captures project requirements up front, feeding directly into the estimation and BOQ process rather than being handled over email and calls.

Multi-step intake wizard capturing space requirements, material tiers, and specifications.

Material Catalog & Pricing Engine

Centralized matrix for interior materials, unit pricing, and labor estimates ensuring consistency across all generated proposals.

Dynamic pricing database with automated volume scaling.

The Result

A faster, more consistent path from client requirements to a usable bill of quantities with automated estimation.

Before

Days Spent Building Manual BOQ Spreadsheets

After

Instant AI-Generated Itemized Cost Estimates

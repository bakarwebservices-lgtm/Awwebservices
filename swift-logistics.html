Live System Operations & Fleet Automation

Swift Logistics

End-to-end operational automation and real-time shipment tracking for enterprise logistics.

60% Reduction in Manual Work
35% On-Time Delivery Lift
Real-Time GPS Dispatch & Tracking

The Problem

Swift Logistics struggled with manual dispatch coordination, fragmented driver updates, and delayed client notifications, resulting in high support ticket volumes and delivery bottlenecks across regional routes.

"Dispatchers spent over half their shift manually calling drivers for updates and updating customer spreadsheets."

The System We Built

Automated Dispatch Engine

Intelligent route assignment and dispatch workflows that cut manual dispatch delays by over 65%.

Custom routing algorithm integrated with real-time driver updates.

Real-Time Fleet & Order Tracking

Integrated tracking dashboard giving dispatchers and end clients live visibility into package movements and estimated delivery times.

WebSockets and Google Maps API integration for live coordinate streaming.

Automated Client Notifications

SMS and Email triggers keeping customers updated at every delivery milestone.

Twilio & SendGrid webhook pipelines for instant alerts.

Centralized Operational CRM

Unified customer shipment history, rate cards, and automated billing generation.

Custom CRM layer with role-based dispatcher access control.

The Result

Reduced manual operational workload by 60%, improved on-time delivery rates by 35%, and provided full transparency to drivers, dispatchers, and customers.
